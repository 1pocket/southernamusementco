Seller questions, answered

The short version of what operators usually ask us first.

Do you buy single machines or only full routes?

Both. Southern Amusement Company buys full routes, partial routes, a handful of locations, or a single piece of commercial-grade amusement equipment in working order.

What areas do you buy routes and equipment in?

West Tennessee and North Mississippi, based out of Jackson, Tennessee. If you are near that footprint, reach out and ask. Phone: (731) 234-9797.

What kind of equipment do you buy?

Coin-operated pool tables (Valley, Dynamo, Diamond, and similar coin-op models), TouchTunes jukeboxes and related equipment, electronic dart boards (Arachnid and similar commercial units), commercial vending machines, and ATMs on location.

How fast can you pick up equipment?

Once we agree on a price, our crew schedules the pickup and handles the move at our expense. For routes with locations, we coordinate a clean handoff with you so no venue is left hanging.

Is the sale confidential?

Yes. Whether you are retiring, consolidating, or exiting a category, we keep the conversation and the deal confidential.
